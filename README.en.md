I'm an undergraduate at the **University of International Business and Economics (UIBE)**, studying Technical Economics and Management in the **AI Innovation and Entrepreneurship experimental program**, with a dual-degree track in management and engineering (2024–2028, expected).

My central question is: **How can AI move beyond answering questions to completing real, complex professional work?** My experience spans financial-agent products and evaluation, data-center supply chains, fixed-income research, and empirical research on the digital economy. I enjoy turning ambiguous requirements into clear questions, then combining data, models, and business judgment into work that can be tested and reviewed.
